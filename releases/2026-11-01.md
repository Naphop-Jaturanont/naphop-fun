# 2026-11-01 · Elevator to Elsewhere

Date: 1 November 2026, Asia/Bangkok. One new original project today.

## Experience

`/elevator/` is a miniature surreal adventure with five illustrated destinations and eight discovery stamps. Carry a borrowed bubble from a fish library to wake a greenhouse, take its luminous flower to a lunar laundromat, then return a clean moon to the lobby to reveal an impossible floor. The final nested-elevator image makes the journey fold back into the passenger's pocket.

All SVG artwork, writing and code are original. No external dependencies, paid services, accounts, API keys, tracking, personal information, persistent storage, company assets or DNS changes. The homepage gains one card; the three previous routes remain unchanged.

## Behavioral study and design hypothesis

Read the public first-party Little Alchemy 2 documentation on [item information](https://help.littlealchemy2.com/general/item-info) and [item types](https://help.littlealchemy2.com/general/item-types). Observed the documented pattern: discoveries remain understandable through item history, while milestones unlock additional possibilities. This is documentation-based behavioral study, not an instrumented trial of that third-party game. No third-party code, artwork, recipes, layout or trademarks are reused.

Hypothesis: a visible carried object, explicit destination hints, and a room that permanently changes after a successful action can make revisiting meaningful without hidden-click hunting. Tested this in the original prototype by visiting rooms out of order, revisiting after transformations, attempting duplicate collection, and completing the whole chain. It is a small design experiment, not evidence from a user study.
