# 2026-11-25 · Half a String

A bilingual listening experiment that also works with audio muted. Pluck 64 cm, predict whether the 32 cm pitch rises, then pluck to reveal. Compare 11 and 22 fundamental cycles in the same 50 ms window. Try 48 cm as a transfer question.

Physics reference: [OpenStax, University Physics Vol. 1 §16.6](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/16-6-standing-waves-and-resonance). For an ideal string fixed at both endpoints, f = v / (2L). With tension and mass per unit length held constant, choose 220 Hz at 64 cm for the model: 32 cm gives 440 Hz and 48 cm gives approximately 293.3 Hz. String motion is illustrative and sound is synthesized, not measured. The wave display isolates the fundamental.

Preflight: run `node tests/half-a-string.cjs` for flow, wrong/right predictions, state guards, focus calls, A/B replay, mute and reset, equal-time wave counts, reduced-motion branch, audio API failure, translations, and portal filter/search. Native browser checks are performed against the deployed site; DOM doubles do not validate physical playback, assistive technology, or actual touch behavior.
