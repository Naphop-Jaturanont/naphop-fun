# 2026-11-02 (Asia/Bangkok) — Gravity Lost & Found

One original daily project: `/gravity/`. Personal Naphop-Jaturanont/naphop-fun only.

## Interaction
Choose an ordinary object, turn its gravity, follow a clue, and wake its secret at the correct edge. Four persistent discoveries unlock a room-sized universe. Wrong direction and premature attempts give feedback instead of progress. Reset replays the room. This is fictional, not a scientific simulation.

## Research and originality
Read PhET's public accessible description of Gravity Force Lab (https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/gravity-force-lab/latest/gravity-force-lab_all.html): controls, state descriptions, and force arrows make cause/effect legible. This was a documentation observation, not a claim to have operated that simulation. Hypothesis tested here: moving only the chosen object makes experiments readable while permanent transformations reward discovery. The setting, artwork, text, mechanics, and code here are original; no PhET or Neal.fun code/assets copied.

Read W3C Dragging Movements guidance (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html). Every action uses native click/tap buttons and keyboard activation; dragging is unnecessary.
