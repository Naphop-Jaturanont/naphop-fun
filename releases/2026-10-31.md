# 2026-10-31 · The Unruly Shadow

Release date: 31 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. One new project today.

## Experience

`/shadow/` is an original, self-contained SVG toy. Move the sun and discover a shadow that lags, naps, wakes, waves, stretches and dances during an eclipse. Thai/English text, keyboard-native range controls, pointer/touch event handling, calm-motion setting, OS reduced-motion preference, reset and a route back to the portal are included. This is imaginative character animation, not an optics simulation.

The portal gains one real card and keeps SA Café Studio and Requirement Detective. No external dependencies, accounts, tracking, storage or network requests are added to the experiment. No custom-domain/DNS changes.
